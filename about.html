About Plan → Do → Launch

Plan → Do → Launch helps SaaS teams make onboarding feel dependable, not exhausting.

Most scaling companies are not short on sales momentum. They are short on post-sale structure.

As complexity rises, onboarding absorbs the strain. Teams rely on a few experienced people, knowledge gets scattered, and leadership gets pulled into issues they should not need to touch.

That pattern is common, and it is solvable with the right operating system.

Plan → Do → Launch is led by John A. McDonald.

John A. McDonald

John is an onboarding strategy consultant focused on high-complexity SaaS and regulated environments.

He has led the design and rollout of onboarding systems supporting more than 50,000 users across compliance, reporting, and financial technology platforms.

His work replaces tribal knowledge and hero-led execution with clear, governed systems teams can actually trust and run at scale.

Over the years, he has built repeatable onboarding frameworks and institutional knowledge models that help teams scale delivery while improving client confidence and adoption.

Plan → Do → Launch packages that experience into a practical operating model built to protect revenue after the sale, not just win it.

The objective is simple: make onboarding durable.

Our Philosophy

If revenue is hard-won, onboarding should protect it.

Onboarding should not depend on memory, improvisation, or who happens to be available.

It should run like infrastructure: structured, visible, measurable, and continuously improving.

When onboarding is engineered intentionally, growth becomes easier to carry.
